# 📊 Power BI HR Analytics & Attrition Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard focused on analyzing employee attrition and workforce dynamics. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of employee distribution, attrition patterns, and key HR metrics, enabling organizations to identify high-risk areas and improve retention strategies.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Track key HR KPIs such as Total Employees, Attrition Count, and Attrition Rate
* Identify departments, job roles, and demographics with high attrition
* Analyze the impact of salary, experience, and job satisfaction on employee turnover
* Support HR decision-making with actionable insights

## 📈 Key Insights

* **Overall Attrition Rate is 16.08%** (238 out of 1480 employees)
* **Research & Development department** has the highest attrition (133 employees), followed by Sales (93)
* **Laboratory Technicians (62)** and **Sales Executives (58)** show the highest attrition among job roles
* **Employees aged 26–35** have the highest attrition (116), indicating early-career turnover
* **Low salary group (Up to 5K)** contributes the most to attrition (163 employees)
* Attrition decreases significantly as salary increases
* Employees with **low job satisfaction (score 2 & 3)** show higher attrition levels
* Majority of attrition comes from employees with **lower experience levels**
* Gender-wise attrition is higher among **male employees (63.45%)** compared to females
